Happy Playtime & Supportive Families

Psychosocial well-being

Lots of play, lots of friends — and happy, helpful homes!

  • Monthly play dates — fun, creative play sessions every month to make friends, play games and express themselves.
  • Parent clubs — monthly meetings and friendly classes where mums and dads share parenting ideas.
2

Open Doors for Everyone

Disability inclusion

Every door, every game and every classroom — open to every child!

  • Fixing play spaces & schools — smooth ramps, handrails and accessible toilets in our buildings.
  • Talking with community leaders — chiefs, church and mosque leaders teach that every child is special, equal and loved.
  • Playing together — children with special needs join right in with every game.

Safe Shield & Helper Teams

Child protection

Grown-ups who keep watch — and help that comes right away!

  • Community safety talks — at schools and neighbourhood centres: how to stay safe and where to get help.
  • Teacher & volunteer training — so grown-ups quickly spot when a child needs help.
  • Caring counselling — gentle support to help children and families heal.
  • Quick helper emergency team — an Emergency Fund, so medical, legal or safe‑haven help comes fast. Help right away!
